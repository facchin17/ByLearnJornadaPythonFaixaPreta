05 · Deliverable D

Cumulative cash & worst drawdown

Operating cash the business throws off, cumulative, before any founder draw. The dip below zero is the capital you must float. With $0 setup and a ~2–3 month payback, this model is not capital-intensive — the risk isn't cash, it's whether the funnel converts (see §Sensitivity, downside stress).

06 · Deliverable C

Full monthly tables

All 24 months for the selected scenario: new closed, active split (entry vs full), MRR, cash flow, cumulative cash, CAC, payback, and team size. Rows where a hire happens are ruled in brass.

CAC = blended (ad spend + referral rewards) ÷ all new closes. Payback = months of survival-weighted client contribution to recover that CAC. Fractional clients are the expected-value math, not half a person.

For the selected scenario, the 3–5 key actions per phase, the single bottleneck that defines the phase, and the trigger for each hire or expansion — a number, never a date. Phases are client-count bands.

Bottom line
