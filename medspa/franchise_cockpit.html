08 · Execution

The operating blueprint

The math only happens if you run the motion. This is how you engineer franchise referrals and keep the two channels compounding.

Engineer the referral (don't wait for it)

The ROI dashboard is the asset

  • The "$X recovered" number is what spreads. Make it undeniable and shareable — screenshot/video, in the owner's own words.
  • Franchise networks have built-in distribution the open market doesn't: owner Facebook/WhatsApp groups, regional meetings, the annual convention, franchisor newsletters, benchmarking calls.
  • Make the ask at the 60–90 day review: (a) a 2-min video testimonial, (b) intros to 3 specific peers, (c) a slot to present at the next owner meeting.
  • Incentive that fits the culture: free month / "founding partner" pricing / small rev-share for intros that close.
  • Endgame: 3–5 winning locations in a brand → the franchisor makes you a preferred vendor → inbound to the whole system, on your terms.
Acquisition mix — referrals AND more ads

They compound, they don't compete

  • Ads land the first anchor. You can't referral your way to location zero — ads + outreach get you the first one.
  • Ads keep the indie base growing so you're never dependent on one account.
  • As referrals cut your blended CAC, ads get more profitable → pour more in. That's the $2.5k→$6k ramp.
  • Target ads at franchise owners: multi-location business owners, franchise-brand interests, franchise-expo audiences, lookalikes of your anchor.
  • Franchise = multi-metro for free. A brand's locations span cities, so the franchise channel sidesteps the single-metro saturation that caps a pure local-ads play.
Guardrail: never let one owner or brand exceed ~25% of MRR. Land the next anchor (different brand) before you cross it.
The sequence — triggers are numbers, not dates
  1. 13–5 undeniable case studies with hard ROI-dashboard numbers. No proof, no anchor.
  2. 2Close anchor #1 (one respected multi-unit operator) — even at a discount. You're buying network access, not just MRR.
  3. 3Hire the Account Manager immediately. 8 locations burn 8 of your 15 solo install slots — you hit the fulfillment ceiling on day one.
  4. 4Harvest the network: owner video + 3 peer intros + a slot at the next owner meeting.
  5. 5Land anchor #2 (different brand) before anchor #1 exceeds 25% of MRR. This is the diversification move that de-risks everything.
  6. 6Hire the media buyer when ad spend crosses $6k — founder-run ads decay above it.
  7. 7Pursue preferred-vendor status only after 3–5 locations in one brand are winning. Then it's inbound.
